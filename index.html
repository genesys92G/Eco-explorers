ORGANIZE · BUILD A FREQUENCY TABLE

Turn your observations into a table

Enter each group and its frequency (count). You will also calculate relative frequency and percent. The relative frequencies should add to 1, and the percentages should add to 100%.

📋 Frequency · f
📘

How to complete this table

Work across each row. Use your sorted observations to find the counts.

  1. Species (group): enter one group on each row. Short names such as Frogs, Orchids, Beetles, and Ferns are okay.
  2. Field symbol: this is a visual reminder. You do not need to type anything here.
  3. Frequency (f): count how many observations are in that group. Add all four frequencies; the total should be 40.
  4. Relative frequency (rf): divide the row’s frequency by the total number of observations. Example: if 6 out of 20 observations are in a group, 6 ÷ 20 = 0.30.
  5. Percent: multiply the relative frequency by 100. In the example, 0.30 × 100 = 30%.
Final check: all frequencies add to 40, relative frequencies add to 1, and percentages add to 100%.
